📱 Android Tablet Installation Guide

Option 1: Instant PWA Install (Recommended — 100% Free Forever)

  1. Connect your Android Tablet to the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
  2. Open Chrome on your tablet and go to: http://192.168.1.3:3000/
  3. Tap the Chrome menu (⋮ in top right corner).
  4. Tap "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".
  5. The app will install as a standalone full-screen app with full S-Pen & touch support!

Option 2: Standalone Android APK

The official ArtCraft suite compiles pure Rust to WebAssembly. You can generate a native .apk wrapper using Bubblewrap or Capacitor:

npx @bubblewrap/cli init --manifest=http://192.168.1.3:3000/manifest.json
npx @bubblewrap/cli build
